The resort

Ice Hills sits on a tea covered hill in Vagamon. Wake up above the mist, walk out into the tea, and watch the Western Ghats fold away into the distance.

Around the resort

Places to visit

Five easy day trips from Ice Hills. No map clicking, no pins — just the places themselves.

Grasslands · about 20 min

Vagamon Meadows

Open rolling meadows for walks, picnics and paragliding above the mist.

Forest walk · about 25 min

Pine Forest

Tall pine avenues and quiet paths. The classic Vagamon walk.

Hill & pilgrimage · about 30 min

Kurisumala

A green hill with a Christian pilgrimage path and wide views over the valley.

Rock & view · about 25 min

Thangalpara

A distinctive rock formation and viewpoint near Kolahalamedu.

Waterfall · about 45 min

Marmala Falls

A wooded waterfall trek near Teekoy, a favourite day trip from the hills.
